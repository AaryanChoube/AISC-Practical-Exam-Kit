PEAS FRAMEWORK FOR AI AGENTS (EXPERIMENT 1)
=================================================
PEAS stands for Performance Measure, Environment, Actuators, Sensors.

1. AUTOMATED / SELF-DRIVING CAR:
   - Performance: Safety, speed, legal drive, passenger comfort, fuel efficiency.
   - Environment: Roads, other vehicles, pedestrians, weather, traffic signs.
   - Actuators: Steering wheel, accelerator, brakes, horn, turn signals.
   - Sensors: Cameras, LiDAR, radar, GPS, speedometer, odometer, engine sensors.
   - Properties: Partially observable, Stochastic, Sequential, Dynamic, Continuous, Multi-agent.

2. MEDICAL DIAGNOSIS SYSTEM:
   - Performance: Healthy patient, minimized treatment costs, diagnostic accuracy.
   - Environment: Patient symptoms, medical history, lab test results, hospital staff.
   - Actuators: Screen display of diagnosis, recommended prescriptions, test orders.
   - Sensors: Keyboard input of symptoms, lab test data feeds.
   - Properties: Partially observable, Stochastic, Sequential, Dynamic, Continuous, Single-agent.

3. VACUUM CLEANER AGENT:
   - Performance: Cleanliness percentage, energy consumed, cleaning time, noise.
   - Environment: Floor surface, dirt locations, obstacles, room boundaries.
   - Actuators: Wheels, brush motor, suction motor.
   - Sensors: Dirt sensor, bump/proximity sensor, infrared cliff sensor.
   - Properties: Partially observable, Deterministic, Episodic/Sequential, Static, Discrete, Single-agent.

4. PART-PICKING ROBOT:
   - Performance: Percentage of parts in correct bins, speed.
   - Environment: Conveyor belt with parts, bins.
   - Actuators: Jointed robotic arm and gripper hand.
   - Sensors: Camera, joint angle sensors.
   - Properties: Partially observable, Stochastic, Episodic, Dynamic, Continuous, Single-agent.
